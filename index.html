Discovering the right molecule shouldn't require testing thousands of the wrong ones.

Molecular discovery is slowed by expensive experiments, fragmented computational workflows, and a limited ability to predict properties early. QYRENOVA builds AI systems that help predict, understand and optimize molecular properties before a compound enters the laboratory.

⏱

Too many molecules, too little time

Millions of candidate molecules can be generated computationally, but evaluating their properties experimentally is costly and slow.

⌬

Property prediction is fragmented

Teams often rely on separate tools and models for individual properties, making workflows complex and hard to compare.

▤

Data isn't fully translated into decisions

Large amounts of chemical and experimental data already exist — turning them into reliable, usable insight remains the hard part.

Our first product

DeepSolv

Give DeepSolv a molecule's structure, and it returns a predicted aqueous solubility. It's built on a chemistry-aware hybrid machine-learning architecture and validated in peer-reviewed research — not just a concept.

Our approach

AI, chemistry and real data — combined, not separate

We combine machine learning, chemistry-aware molecular representations and real experimental data to develop predictive models designed to support molecular decision-making.

Machine learning

Models that learn the relationship between molecular structure and measured properties.

Chemical knowledge

Chemistry-aware molecular representations and features, not generic embeddings.

Real-world data

Curated experimental data, so predictions are grounded in measured chemistry.

Actionable insight

Predictions framed to support a decision — which molecule to test next.
